Material anatomy

Four layers, one surface.

Pigmented body → fine grain → diffuse edge light → contact and cast shadow. The label lives above all four.

Type & rhythm

Aa Sea glass

Clear, calm, familiar.

System sans for controls. Editorial serif for this studio only. A 4 px spacing rhythm, 44 px minimum targets, 48 px standard controls.

Touch & motion

Weight without delay.

Press settles in 160 ms. Selection glides in 260 ms; expanding surfaces settle in 340 ms. Motion follows your action and can be interrupted. Reduced motion removes animation.

Native translation

Kotlin / ComposeSwift / SwiftUI

Translate the intent.

Share pigment, radii, elevation and state tokens. Preserve platform gestures, accessibility and native presentations. Grain can be drawn from a tiny shared tile.
